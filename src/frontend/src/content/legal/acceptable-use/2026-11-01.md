This Acceptable Use Policy sets out what you may and may not do with Bonza. It forms part of our
[Terms of Service](/legal/terms) and is a draft pending review by qualified legal counsel.

## Permitted use

Bonza is for planning and booking your own travel, and that of people you are booking for. You may compare
options, redeem your own loyalty points, and book cash travel through the service.

## Prohibited use

You must not:

- **Scrape, crawl or harvest** award availability, pricing, or any other content from Bonza, whether by
  automated means or manually at scale, for storage, redistribution or resale.
- **Query the service automatically** — through bots, scripts, or headless browsers — beyond ordinary personal
  use, or in a way that places unreasonable load on our systems or our suppliers' systems.
- **Resell, republish or commercially exploit** award-availability data, point valuations, or any other
  information Bonza surfaces. This data is licensed from third parties and aggregated at our cost; it is not
  yours to redistribute.
- **Circumvent** rate limits, access controls, or the consent gate, or attempt to access another user's
  account or data.
- **Misrepresent** who you are, create accounts by automated means, or open multiple accounts to abuse the
  Bonza Credits or first-booking offers.
- **Interfere** with the service's security or availability, including probing, scanning, or attempting to
  defeat authentication other than through our responsible-disclosure route.

## Enforcement

We may rate-limit, suspend or terminate accounts that breach this policy, and may remove content or withdraw
access without notice where a breach threatens the service or our suppliers. Termination for breach forfeits
any outstanding Bonza Credits (see [Terms](/legal/terms)).

## Security disclosure

If you find a vulnerability, report it to **security@bonza.app** rather than exploiting or publicising it. We
will not pursue good-faith research conducted under that route.

## Changes

We may update this policy; every version is kept and addressable (see [version history](/legal/acceptable-use/changes)).
