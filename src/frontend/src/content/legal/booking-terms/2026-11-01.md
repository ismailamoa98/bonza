These Booking Terms apply when you book cash travel through Bonza. They sit alongside our
[Terms of Service](/legal/terms) and the travel provider's own conditions, and are a draft pending review by
qualified legal counsel. They do **not** apply to points redemptions, which are contracts between you and the
loyalty programme (see [Terms §Points redemptions](/legal/terms#points-agency)).

## Who you are contracting with

When you book cash travel, Bonza facilitates the booking and takes payment. **The flight is booked and
ticketed in-app through our flight supplier; hotels and cars are booked on the provider's own site via a
deep link.** The travel provider's own terms — fares, baggage, check-in, changes and cancellations — apply to
the travel itself, and we show you who the provider is before you pay. (Whether Bonza acts as the supplier's
agent or as merchant is being confirmed with counsel and will be stated here precisely before launch.)

## Payment

You pay the total shown, in the currency displayed, by card through our payment processor (Stripe). Payment is
taken when you confirm. We use 3-D Secure (SCA) on every card payment. The amount on your statement appears
under a `BONZA` descriptor.

## Confirmation

Your booking is confirmed only when you receive a confirmation from us (for flights) or from the provider (for
hotels and cars). If a price or availability has changed between search and payment — our prices are a
point-in-time cache — we will show you the current price before charging, and you are free to decline.

## Refunds and cancellations {#refunds}

**Refunds and cancellations are governed by the travel provider's own fare or rate rules**, which we show you
before you book. Many cheaper fares and rates are non-refundable or carry change fees — check the conditions
before you pay.

- **To cancel or change a flight** booked in-app, contact Bonza support and we will process it with the
  supplier under the fare rules; any supplier refund is returned to your original payment method.
- **To cancel or change a hotel or car** booked on the provider's site, deal with the provider directly under
  the terms you accepted there.
- Where you have a statutory right to cancel (for example under the Consumer Contracts Regulations), that right
  is unaffected — but note it generally does **not** apply to travel for a specified date (see
  [Terms §Your consumer rights](/legal/terms)).
- Any refund reverses the related Bonza Credits earned on that booking.

If the provider becomes insolvent, your protection depends on the booking type and any applicable scheme; we
will set out the position clearly once our regulatory status is finalised.

## Bonza Credits on cash bookings

You earn 3% back in Bonza Credits on the cash component, credited after the provider's cancellation window
closes. Full credit terms are in the [Terms of Service](/legal/terms).

## Changes

We may update these terms; every version is kept and addressable (see [version history](/legal/booking-terms/changes)).
Material changes require your re-acceptance at next login.
