This Cookie Policy explains the cookies and similar technologies Bonza uses, and how you control them. It is a
draft pending review by qualified legal counsel. For how we handle personal data more broadly, see our
[Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy).

## How we ask for consent

When you first visit Bonza we show a consent banner with equal **Accept all** and **Reject all** choices, plus
a **Choose** option for per-category control. **Nothing beyond strictly-necessary cookies is set until you
choose**, and rejecting leaves every part of the site usable — there is no cookie wall. Your choice is
remembered for **12 months**, after which we ask again. You can reopen the preferences at any time from
**Privacy settings** in the footer.

## Categories

- **Strictly necessary** (always on) — authentication session, security, load balancing, and remembering your
  consent choice itself. These do not require consent because the service cannot run without them.
- **Functional** (consent) — remembering preferences such as your home airport and recently viewed trips.
- **Analytics** (consent) — PostHog (EU cloud) product analytics, so we can see which features are used and
  fix what is broken. Withdrawing analytics consent stops collection in the same session and clears the
  PostHog cookies.
- **Marketing** (consent) — measuring and tailoring any promotional messaging. Off unless you opt in.

## Managing cookies

You control categories through our banner and the **Privacy settings** link. You can also block or delete
cookies in your browser settings, though strictly-necessary cookies may be reset on your next visit because
they are required for the site to function.

We may update this policy; every version is kept and addressable (see [version history](/legal/cookies/changes)).
