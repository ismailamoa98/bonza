This Privacy Policy explains how Bonza ("we", "us") collects, uses and protects your personal data. It is a
draft pending review by qualified legal counsel and completion with our registered entity, ICO registration
number and contact details.

## Who we are

Bonza is a UK-based AI travel optimiser that helps you book flights, hotels and cars for the best combination
of cash and loyalty points. We are the data controller for the personal data described here. For any privacy
question or to exercise your rights, contact **privacy@bonza.app**.

## Data we collect

- **Account** — your name and email (managed through our authentication provider, Clerk), your home airport
  and travel preferences.
- **Loyalty data** — programme balances and status, either entered manually or inferred from statement emails
  (see below).
- **Trip activity** — searches, saved trips and bookings.
- **Payment data** — processed by Stripe; we never store full card numbers.
- **Usage and device data** — aggregated analytics (only with your consent) and basic device information.

## Email statement sync

If you connect Gmail or Outlook, Bonza reads your mailbox with a **read-only** scope (`gmail.readonly`, or the
Microsoft Graph `Mail.Read` equivalent), filters to messages from a fixed allowlist of loyalty-programme sender
domains, and extracts **only** the programme identity, the balance figure and the statement date. The raw
message body is **discarded and never persisted** — only the extracted balance record is stored.

Because of this, your balances are **inferred from statements, not retrieved from the programme**. They can
therefore lag or differ from your live balance, which is why Bonza shows a discrepancy notice when a newer
statement disagrees with what we hold. Bonza **never sends, deletes or modifies** your mail, and you can
disconnect at any time, which revokes our access token immediately.

## Automated decision-making

Bonza's redemption engine ranks your options and our personalisation chooses destinations to suggest. This is
profiling under Article 22(1) UK GDPR, but it produces **no legal or similarly significant effect** — it is a
ranking of travel options you remain free to ignore — so the Article 22 prohibition does not apply. The logic
is straightforward: your loyalty balances, award availability, cash fares, and our maintained point
valuations. You can object to this processing at any time by emailing **privacy@bonza.app**, and you can
always book outside any recommendation.

## Data residency

Our application database (RDS) and file storage (S3) are hosted in an EU/UK region; product analytics use
PostHog's **EU cloud**; error monitoring uses Sentry's **EU region**. Some sub-processors (Duffel, Stripe,
Clerk, Seats.aero, Resend) process data under their own transfer mechanisms (UK adequacy, UK IDTA or SCCs) —
each is listed, with its mechanism and DPA, on our [Sub-processors page](/legal/subprocessors).

## Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict and port your data, and to object to processing. You
can **export** all your data and **delete** your account directly from **Settings → Privacy** (export produces
a machine-readable archive; delete starts a 14-day window with an undo). Alternatively, email
**privacy@bonza.app**. We respond within **one month**, extendable by two further months for complex requests,
and we will tell you if we extend.

## Financial-record retention

Deleting your account does **not** erase completed financial records. UK tax law requires us to keep booking
and credit-ledger records for six years after the end of the relevant accounting period; we keep them for
**seven years**, with your personal fields pseudonymised (replaced by a non-identifying token) and only the
financial figures retained. This is the Article 17(3)(b) legal-obligation exemption.

## Children

Bonza is for adults only (**18+**). We do not knowingly process the data of under-18s; accounts found to
belong to under-18s are terminated on discovery. This matters because the service handles payment and travel
booking.

We may update this policy; every version is kept and addressable (see [version history](/legal/privacy/changes)).
Material changes are notified by email.
