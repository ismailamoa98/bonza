These Bonza Pro Terms govern the optional Bonza Pro subscription. They form part of our
[Terms of Service](/legal/terms) and are a draft pending review by qualified legal counsel.

## What Pro unlocks

Bonza Pro unlocks the advanced redemption tools — cross-programme transfer optimisation, award search across
partners, and ongoing Bonza Credits beyond your first free booking. The free tier remains fully usable for
search and cash booking; Pro is an enhancement, not a paywall over the core service.

## Price and billing

Bonza Pro costs **£49.99 per year**, billed in advance to your card through Stripe when you subscribe. The
price shown at purchase is the price you pay for that term. VAT is included where applicable.

## Renewal

Your subscription **renews automatically** each year at the then-current price unless you cancel before the
renewal date. We remind you by email before each renewal. If we change the price, we tell you in advance and
the new price applies only from your next renewal — never mid-term.

## Cancellation

You can cancel at any time from **Settings → Pro**. On cancellation:

- You keep Pro access until the **end of the period you have already paid for** — we do not cut you off
  mid-term — and it does not renew after that.
- Your **14-day cancellation right** under the Consumer Contracts Regulations applies from the day you
  subscribe. If you cancel within 14 days and have not used Pro, you get a full refund; if you have used it, we
  may deduct a proportionate amount for the use you have had (see
  [Terms §Your consumer rights](/legal/terms)).

## Downgrade

When Pro ends, Pro-only tools stop being available and you return to the free tier. Bonza Credits already
earned and not expired remain yours, subject to the credit rules in the [Terms of Service](/legal/terms).

## Changes

We may update these terms; every version is kept and addressable (see [version history](/legal/pro-terms/changes)).
Material changes require your re-acceptance at next login.
