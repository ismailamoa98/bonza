Bonza uses the sub-processors below to run the service. Each processes personal data on our behalf under a
data processing agreement. This page is a draft pending review by qualified legal counsel and may be updated
as our suppliers change — **adding a sub-processor triggers a notice email but does not require you to
re-accept our [Terms](/legal/terms)**. For how we handle your data overall, see the
[Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy).

## Current sub-processors

| Sub-processor | Purpose | Data categories | Location | Transfer mechanism | DPA |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Amazon Web Services** | Application hosting, database (RDS), file storage (S3) | All stored account, trip and booking data | EU/UK region | UK adequacy (EU) | [AWS DPA](https://aws.amazon.com/compliance/data-privacy/) |
| **Clerk** | Authentication and identity | Name, email, auth metadata | US | UK IDTA / SCCs | [Clerk DPA](https://clerk.com/legal/dpa) |
| **Stripe** | Payment processing | Name, email, card token, billing address | US / EU | UK adequacy (EU entity) / SCCs | [Stripe DPA](https://stripe.com/legal/dpa) |
| **Duffel** | Flight and accommodation booking | Passenger name, contact, travel details | UK / EU | UK domestic / adequacy | [Duffel DPA](https://duffel.com/legal) |
| **Seats.aero** | Award-availability data | Search parameters (no direct identifiers) | US | SCCs | Per supplier agreement |
| **PostHog** | Product analytics (consent-gated) | Pseudonymous usage events, device data | EU cloud | Within EU | [PostHog DPA](https://posthog.com/handbook/company/security) |
| **Sentry** | Error monitoring | Error context, pseudonymous identifiers (no PII) | EU region | Within EU | [Sentry DPA](https://sentry.io/legal/dpa/) |
| **Resend** | Transactional and (consent-gated) marketing email | Name, email | US / EU | SCCs | [Resend DPA](https://resend.com/legal/dpa) |

Analytics (PostHog) and marketing email run only with your consent. Error monitoring (Sentry) operates on
legitimate interest with personally identifying information suppressed.
