## What Bonza is

Bonza is an optimisation and booking-facilitation service. We help you compare and book travel using the best
mix of cash and loyalty points, and we explain the trade-offs. We are not the travel provider.

## Points redemptions — agency {#points-agency}

When you redeem loyalty points, **Bonza surfaces award availability and links you to the programme's own
booking surface. The resulting redemption is a contract between you and the airline, hotel or loyalty
programme.** Bonza is not a party to it, receives no booking record, and **cannot view, change, cancel or
refund it.**

Availability and pricing we show are a **point-in-time cache** and may differ by the time you reach the
programme's site. Bonza does **not guarantee** award space, transfer times between programmes, or that a
displayed transfer ratio will still apply when a transfer executes. Always confirm the final terms on the
programme's own site before you transfer points or book.

## Bonza Credits

You earn 3% back in Bonza Credits on the **cash component** of cash bookings. Credits are issued **after the
supplier's cancellation window closes** (not at booking), have **no cash value**, expire per the published
rule, and are **forfeited if your account is terminated for breach**. We may vary the rate **prospectively**.
